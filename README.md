## Designing a Health & Fitness Tracking App
## Project Title: FitLife – Personalized Health Companion
## Problem Statement:
Many individuals struggle to maintain a consistent fitness routine and track their health progress due to complex interfaces, scattered data (from wearables, diet logs, etc.), and lack of motivation.

## Target Users:

Young professionals aged 20–40 with busy schedules
Fitness beginners who need guidance
Health-conscious users managing diet and activity levels

## Goals:

Simplify tracking of daily activity, diet, and sleep
Provide personalized workout and nutrition plans
Offer motivational feedback and progress insights

## Key UX Design Challenges:

Balancing simplicity with depth of data (avoid cluttered dashboards)
Designing engaging visual feedback (progress rings, achievements, streaks)
Integrating data from wearable devices seamlessly
Ensuring accessibility and readability during workouts (e.g., large fonts, contrast)

## Deliverables:

User personas and journey maps
Wireframes for onboarding, daily dashboard, and progress summary
Interactive prototype in Figma
Usability test results and design iteration summary
